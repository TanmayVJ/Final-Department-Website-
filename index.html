About This Portal

The Heart of India, Digitally Connected

This portal is a unified digital gateway to the Government of Madhya Pradesh — designed to give every citizen seamless access to state departments, welfare schemes, and government services from a single place.

Madhya Pradesh, with its 52 districts and 85 million people, is India's second-largest state by area. The state government operates across 30+ departments delivering services in health, education, agriculture, infrastructure, and social welfare.

From the 2000 bifurcation that created Chhattisgarh, to becoming the nation's wheat bowl and the leader in tiger conservation — MP's governance story is one of constant evolution and ambition for its people.

1956
Madhya Pradesh formed on November 1st as States Reorganisation Act enacted. Bhopal becomes state capital.
2000
Chhattisgarh carved out as separate state from MP's southern districts under the MP Reorganisation Act.
2007
MP launches Ladli Laxmi Yojana — a pioneering girl child welfare scheme, later adopted by other states nationwide.
2012
MP achieves "Krishi Karman Award" for record wheat production — a recognition repeated multiple times thereafter.
2023–24
Launch of CM Ladli Bahna Yojana & Seekho Kamao Yojana — transformative schemes benefiting crores of citizens.
